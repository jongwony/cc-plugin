# Gathering

Read at steps 1 and 2 of the procedure. Establishing what is the case is `/inquire`'s; this file says what the brief needs established and what counts as a candidate.

## What the brief needs established

As of the brief's date and time:

- **What the user is responsible for**, and what their organization is pushing on now — enough to rank the user's projects by what matters most. The ranking settles only after every connected source has been read: the busiest source is not the most important one.
- **Every connected source**, read back until the asks addressed to the user and the user's own commitments turn up closed, and ahead as far as the brief shows (`selection.md` §Shape). Candidates are found by what is open — an unanswered ask, an unkept commitment — never by read or seen marks, nor by where a page of results cuts off. Updates are sought by what the user's responsibilities depend on, not by where the user speaks.
- **The candidates** below, each with its source.

Of what `/inquire` hands back as the user's, only a decision someone is waiting on in a record is a to-do candidate, naming the decision and who is waiting. An uncertainty about this run's own reading is settled by the rules here or named in the brief's closing line, as is what could not be reached, with what was tried. Unattended, no turn waits on an answer.

## Candidates

A candidate is anything that might be a to-do or an update:

- a to-do may come from something the user committed to, something asked of them, something an occasion requires of them, or a duty they hold without anyone asking;
- an update is news, not the user's own act or message, that bears on their projects or their organization's priorities. An action that a piece of news implies for the user is a candidate of its own.

A candidate's source is the record where its current state lives and where the user would act on it, not the message or notification that pointed there.

Every candidate is recorded with its source and ends with a verdict and its reason: to-do, open but not today, update, push forward, or drop. A verdict given before the checks is provisional: a candidate leaves the to-do path only on its check. Every candidate whose verdict places it is shown, and a section of candidates holds no others.
