# Visual page

Read at step 6 of the procedure. The page lowers the reader's load by showing as pictures the relations the brief's text states, so they are taken in at a glance rather than assembled from sentences.

## What a figure shows

A figure appears only where it carries a relation the brief's text states, such as:

- **Time**: today's meetings, and the deadlines and triggers of the to-dos, placed on one time axis.
- **Waiting**: who waits on whom, and for which item.
- **Grouping**: items grouped by their area.

Every fact in a figure comes from a verified item in the brief, and each figure's elements point back to those items. A relation the text does not state gets no figure, and no figure is decorative. The text of the brief stays on the page beside its figures, in `<language>`, labels included.

## Publishing

- Before writing the page, load the host's artifact design guidance (the artifact-design skill or the Artifact tool's quickstart) and follow it.
- One artifact serves every run. Its URL is kept in `<dir>/artifact-url`. Where that file exists, republish to that URL. Where it does not, the first publish creates the artifact and writes its URL there.
- The artifact stays private. The run does not share it.
