# Remaining work — what a session still holds open

- **Remaining work is what lives only in the conversation.** An item deliberately handed to the unit's chart is the chart's from then on: later work meets it there through chart search, and it is not announced again. Work the session was given stays the session's until it is done; writing it on the chart discharges nothing.
- **An open item or a debt is settled in the session first; one the session will not close moves to the unit's chart without asking, and the report names what moved.** The session is the fast layer and Linear the slow one. A session with no chart holds its debts in the session. This governs what is still open; a direction choi sets is logged when it changes (`/unfold decide`).
- **A commitment with a deadline, when handed to a chart, names what brings it back before that date.**
- **What is open is read out when choi asks for it**, through the `residue` skill. A reply carries what its own turn changed, not a recurring block of unchanged state.
