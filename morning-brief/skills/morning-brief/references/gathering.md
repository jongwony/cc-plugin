# Gathering

Read at steps 1 and 2 of the procedure. Establishing what is the case is `/inquire`'s.

## What the brief needs established

- **What the user is responsible for**; what that work depends on — the people, upstream projects and services whose change would change it; and what whoever sets its direction, who may be the user alone, is pushing on now. The user's projects are ranked from these only after every connected source has been read: the busiest source is not the most important one.
- **What is still open to the user.** Each tool is asked for it directly wherever it can answer, with no date bound; a search the tool supports for the user's own messages, or for messages to them with no reply, counts. A tool that cannot be asked is read back until every exchange where the user was asked or committed to something, and has not since replied, is covered, whatever its date. Where that cannot be completed, the closing names the date reached. The previous brief supplies candidates and never bounds the read.
- **Updates**, sought by what the user's work depends on, not by where the user speaks.
- **The calendar**, to a fixed horizon: the next day, and on the week's first brief the rest of the week. Dated events in the user's own records count as occasions too.

No turn waits on an answer before the brief is saved: `/inquire`'s questions are collected for the closing, never asked mid-run. Of what it hands back as the user's, only a decision someone is waiting on in a record is a candidate. So does an uncertainty about this run's own reading that these rules do not settle.

## Candidates

A candidate is anything that might be the user's open act (`selection.md` §To-do) or an update: news that bears on their projects or on the direction of their work. News and the action it implies are separate candidates, each with its own verdict.

A candidate's source is the record where its current state lives and where the user would act on it, not the message that pointed there.

Every candidate is recorded with its source and ends with a verdict and its reason: to-do, push forward, open but not today, update, or drop. A verdict given before the checks is provisional. Every candidate whose verdict places it is shown, and a section of candidates holds no others.
