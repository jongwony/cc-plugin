# Visual page

Read at step 6 of the procedure. The page lowers the reader's load by showing as pictures the relations the brief's text states, so they are taken in at a glance rather than assembled from sentences.

## What a figure shows

A figure is drawn only for a relation the brief's text states and its layout does not already show, such as:

- **Time**: today's meetings, and the deadlines and triggers of the to-dos, placed on one time axis.
- **Waiting**: who waits on whom, and for which item.

Every fact in a figure comes from a verified item in the brief, and each figure's elements point back to the item they came from. No figure is decorative. The page's text is the self-checked markdown brief, in `<language>`, labels included.

Before publishing, apply step 5's self-check to the figures as well: each figure fact matches the item it points to, and points to the right one.

## Publishing

- Before writing the page, load the host's artifact design guidance (the artifact-design skill or the Artifact tool's quickstart) and follow it.
- One artifact serves every run. Its URL is kept in `<dir>/artifact-url`. Where that file exists, read that artifact with the Artifact tool first, then republish to its URL. Where it does not, the first publish creates the artifact and writes its URL there.
- The artifact stays private. The run does not share it.
